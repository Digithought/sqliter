## Why these are pre-existing

- `docs/invariants.md`, `docs/runtime.md`, `docs/sync.md` — **not touched** by this ticket
  (`git diff --stat` covers only `docs/usage.md`, `docs/module-authoring.md`, two source files
  and two spec files). Their word counts are byte-identical to `HEAD`:
  `git show HEAD:docs/runtime.md` counts 13422, the same as the working tree; `docs/sync.md`
  counts 13830 both ways. Both were already above their recorded ratchet before this ticket.
- `docs/module-authoring.md` was **already over the 12000-word cap at `HEAD`** —
  `git show HEAD:docs/module-authoring.md` counts 12145 — so this check failed there too.
  Disclosure: this ticket added ~200 words to it (a required third subsection in
  § *Row-Shape, Table-Name, and Row-Key Contract Across Mid-Transaction ALTER*, documenting
  the new `key` guarantee), taking it to 12345. The failure is not new; the overshoot grew.
  Whoever resolves this should treat the doc as needing a split, not a ratchet bump.
