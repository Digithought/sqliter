## Why this is pre-existing

Ticket `debt-filter-equality-facts-ignore-semantic-ordering` touched four docs —
`docs/types.md`, `docs/optimizer-fd.md`, `docs/invariants.md`, and
`docs/optimizer-rule-families.md` (the last only to repoint one link to a retitled
section). `git status --porcelain` shows none of the three failing files is modified in
this working tree, so `docs:check` reports them at their committed HEAD content. None of
the four docs this ticket did edit is reported — the link/anchor half of `docs:check`
passes clean, including the two new anchors this ticket added.

The three failures are word-count ratchet violations only: some earlier change grew
`schema.md` (+343 words over its recorded ratchet) and `sync.md` (+680), and
`module-authoring.md` crossed the 12000-word cap for a doc that has no ratchet recorded.
Fixing them means either trimming/splitting the prose or re-recording the ratchets with
`--update-ratchet --force` plus a justification in the commit message — a decision about
those three documents, unrelated to the optimizer FD/EC gate this ticket implements.
